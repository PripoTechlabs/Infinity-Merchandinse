# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B procurement managers, entrepreneurs, and supply-chain leads in GCC, EU, US, and Asia markets. They evaluate suppliers at a desk — comparing credibility, range, and reliability. They need to feel confident they're looking at a partner who handles serious volume and delivers.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Infinity Merchandise, a Dubai-based global sourcing and trading company. Covers 14 product categories across international markets. Goal: generate qualified enquiry leads and establish immediate credibility as a capable, ambitious trading partner.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, connected, direct. Confident without being stiff. Speaks equally to the solo entrepreneur and the procurement head at a regional chain. No corporate padding — get to the point.

## Anti-references

- Generic Dubai corporate: navy + gold + Arial — every trading company in the GCC looks like this
- SaaS startup: glassmorphism, neon accents, hero metrics — tech aesthetics on a physical goods business
- Low-budget supplier: Aliexpress/wholesale vibes, cheap product grids, wall of text

## Design Principles

1. **Earned restraint**: Space is used intentionally — not as decoration, but to give weight to what matters.
2. **Direct voice**: No intro sentences that restate the heading. Every word is load-bearing.
3. **Scale as hierarchy**: Big things are much bigger than medium things. Flat scales are timid.
4. **Committed palette**: One bold color carries the surface. Hedging with neutrals is cowardice.
5. **Brand through structure**: The grid and type do the brand work — not effects, textures, or gradients.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. 4.5:1 minimum contrast on body text, 3:1 on large type. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
