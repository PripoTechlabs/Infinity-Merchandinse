# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B buyers — procurement managers at SMEs and enterprises, primarily in the Gulf region and international markets. They arrive with intent: sourcing a specific category or evaluating a trading partner. Context is professional, decision-making, often delegated research.

## Product Purpose

Infinity Merchandise is a Dubai-based global sourcing and trading company. The site exists to establish credibility, communicate range, and convert visitors into enquiries. Success means a filled enquiry form or a direct contact.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, global. The brand should feel like a trusted senior partner — not a marketplace, not a catalogue site. Confident without being loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic trading-directory sites (Alibaba-style busy grids, marketplace clutter)
- Generic agency templates (stock photo heroes, pastel cards, bland sans-serif)
- Corporate consulting grey (McKinsey-flat navy-white with zero personality)

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility first** — every element should reinforce trust and expertise, not decoration.
2. **Let the range speak** — product breadth is a selling point; show it without overwhelming.
3. **Friction-free enquiry** — the path to contact should always be visible and one step away.
4. **Global grounding** — Dubai-headquartered, worldwide reach; design should feel international, not regional.
5. **Restraint as confidence** — a company that knows its value doesn't need to shout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Keyboard navigable. High contrast maintained across all interactive elements.
